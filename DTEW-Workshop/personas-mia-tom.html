PERSONA — Mia

Age 20
Job Student
Status Single
Location Mid-size city
Archetype The Everyperson
M

Quote

"I'm informed — I scroll through the news every day."

Motivation

Wants to feel informed and keep up with what her friends are talking about, without much effort.

Goals

Stay reasonably up to date on current events; not fall behind in conversations with friends and family.

Pain points / Frustration

Doesn't realize her feed mostly confirms what she already thinks. Occasionally caught off guard when friends hold a very different opinion.

Short bio

Checks Instagram/TikTok multiple times a day, mostly following accounts that already match her views. Sees herself as informed and open-minded but has never actively sought a differing opinion.

PERSONA — Tom

Age 22
Job Student (part-time job)
Status Single
Location Mid-size city
Archetype The Seeker
T

Quote

"I don't want my feed to just tell me I'm right."

Motivation

Wants a genuinely balanced view of current events and to avoid being manipulated by an algorithm.

Goals

Actively break out of his filter bubble; understand other perspectives without having to dig for them manually.

Pain points / Frustration

Actively searches for other viewpoints, but the algorithm keeps pulling him back to similar content. Manual searching feels tedious.

Short bio

More media-literate than average, follows a handful of accounts on purpose to diversify his feed, but feels like he's fighting the algorithm rather than being helped by it.
