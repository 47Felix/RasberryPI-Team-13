ⓘ What is this?

One feed, two ranking modes for the same seed post: Standard sorts purely by content similarity - bubble-reinforcing, like a typical "For You" feed. Diversity-aware deliberately mixes in topically related counter-perspectives. Switch between the two and feel the difference while scrolling, not just as a single number. Prototype for Case 3 (DTEW Hamburg 2026, digi&demo e.V.).
