## Persona 1 – Mia

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 20 |
| **Job** | Student (undecided major, first year) |
| **Status** | Single, lives in a shared flat |
| **Location** | Mid-size city |
| **Archetype** | "The Everyperson", average, unreflective media user |

**Quote:** *"I'm informed, I scroll through the news every day."*

**Motivation:** Wants to feel informed and keep up with what her friends are talking about, without much effort.

**Goals:** Stay reasonably up to date on current events; not fall behind in conversations with friends and family.

**Pain points / frustration:** Doesn't realize her feed mostly confirms what she already thinks. Occasionally caught off guard when friends hold a very different opinion, briefly unsettling, but she doesn't connect it to her feed.

**Short bio:** Mia checks Instagram and TikTok multiple times a day, mostly following accounts and topics that already match her views. She sees herself as informed and open-minded but has never actively sought out a differing opinion; she simply doesn't encounter the need to.

## Persona 2 – Tom

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 22 |
| **Job** | Student (working part-time) |
| **Status** | Single, lives alone |
| **Location** | Mid-size city |
| **Archetype** | "The Seeker", actively questions his own information diet |

**Quote:** *"I don't want my feed to just tell me I'm right."*

**Motivation:** Wants a genuinely balanced view of current events and to avoid being manipulated by an algorithm.

**Goals:** Actively break out of his filter bubble; understand other political/social perspectives without having to dig for them manually.

**Pain points / frustration:** Has noticed his feed is one-sided and actively searches for other viewpoints, but the algorithm keeps pulling him back to similar content. Manually searching for counter-opinions feels tedious and he often gives up.

**Short bio:** Tom is more media-literate than average and follows a handful of accounts on purpose to diversify his feed, but feels like he's fighting the algorithm rather than being helped by it. He's open to a tool that would do this work for him, as long as it stays relevant and doesn't flood him with irrelevant content.

**Personality** (Introverted↔Extroverted / Thinking↔Feeling / Free↔Dependent / Social↔Ego):
- Introverted ○○○○○●●●●○ Extroverted *(leans extroverted, ~70%)*
- Thinking ●●●●●●●○○○ Feeling *(leans thinking, ~35%)*
- Free ○○●●●●●●●○ Dependent *(leans free, ~20%)*
- Social ●●●●●○○○○○ Ego *(balanced, slightly social, ~50%)*

**Technology:**
- IT & Internet: ●●●●●●●●●○ (comfortable, technically curious)
- Software: ●●●●●●○○○○ (uses browser extensions, some tools beyond defaults)
- Mobile Apps: ●●●●●●●○○○ (moderate, prefers desktop/browser)
- Social Networks: ●●●●●●●○○○ (active but more selective than Mia)

**Brands:** X (Twitter), Reddit, RSS readers / news aggregators
