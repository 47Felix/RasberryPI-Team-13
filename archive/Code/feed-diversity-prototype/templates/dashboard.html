ⓘ What is this?

Shows, for each account, what feed bias it currently has and where it comes from - "likes/comments" (real engagement, always wins once it exists) or "survey" (sign-up survey at registration, only as long as no engagement on that topic exists yet). Each topic chip states the concrete position instead of just "pro"/"contra" (e.g. "climate ▲ · more climate protection, faster"), so the label reads clearly without the post next to it. Shows only your own logged-in account by default ("Just me") - "All accounts" shows the whole team for comparison. The reasoning for a single post in your own feed is shown right on the post itself (ⓘ line). Only visible with a valid token, see ADMIN_DASHBOARD_TOKEN in .env.
